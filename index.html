What sets me apart

I blend creativity with technical precision, so every video meets the brief and then goes further.

I'm a video editor with 3 years of professional experience, working across high-retention short-form reels, immersive long-form documentaries and polished podcasts.

I keep post-production seamless, moving between the Adobe Suite and AI generation tools to hit the exact tone and pacing a project needs. A background in photography gives me a sharp eye for composition, lighting and framing in every cut.

Real estate tours, educational finance content, infotainment documentaries: whatever the format, the goal stays the same. Keep the audience hooked.
